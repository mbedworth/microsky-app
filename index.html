Getting help

Questions, problems and ideas are welcome. Open a request on MicroSky's support page on GitHub, or, if you are testing MicroSky, send feedback from the TestFlight app.

Common questions

Where does the weather come from?
Apple Weather and the national weather services: the US National Weather Service, Environment and Climate Change Canada and Germany's Deutscher Wetterdienst, with Open-Meteo and MET Norway elsewhere. Each forecast says which sources it used, under the info button.

How do I connect my weather station?
In Settings, choose your station's service (Tempest, Davis WeatherLink, Ambient Weather, Ecowitt or Weather Underground) and enter the token or keys from that service. They are kept in your device's Keychain and sent only to that service.

Why does the sky turn when I turn my iPhone?
With the Horizon style, the sky faces the way your iPhone looks, so the sun, moon and stars are where you would see them. You can turn this off in Settings, and Reduce Motion holds the view still.

Can I change the units?
Yes. Settings has a choice for each kind of reading, and buttons to switch everything to Imperial or Metric at once.
